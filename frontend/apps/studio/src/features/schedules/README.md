# schedules

## Overview

Schedules (feature 018): listing the schedules of a namespace, creating and editing one, and, on its page, what the platform has decided for it: the next times, every decision it made, its backfills, and the way to pause, resume, run now, backfill or delete it.

## Interface

- `SchedulesPage` lists the schedules with their state, workflow, rule and next time, which the person can narrow by state, workflow and the start of a name (the platform filters; the name is asked for once typing has rested); `SchedulePage` shows one schedule, its next times as the platform computed them, and holds `ScheduleControls`, `Decisions` and `Backfills`. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/SchedulesPage.tsx:SchedulesPage] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/SchedulePage.tsx:SchedulePage]
- `ScheduleFormPage` is the form at `/schedules/new` and `/schedules/:scheduleId/edit`. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleFormPage.tsx:ScheduleFormPage]
- `ScheduleControls` offers pause, resume, run now, edit and delete, each behind the permissions the platform asks for. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleControls.tsx:ScheduleControls]
- `Decisions` pages through the decisions of a schedule, newest first, optionally of one outcome; `Backfills` lists, starts and cancels backfills. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/Decisions.tsx:Decisions] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/Backfills.tsx:Backfills]
- `model` holds the pure rules: `requestOf` (the request a draft makes, with every check), `draftOf` (the draft that edits a schedule, or why it cannot), `describeSpec`, `scheduleState`/`outcomeView`/`reasonText` (words and shapes), `backfillOf` and `noteProblem`. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:requestOf] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:draftOf] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:backfillOf]
- `useCommand` sends one command at a time under a stable idempotency key; `useNamespaceId` resolves the slug the Studio holds to the id the routes carry. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/shared.tsx:useCommand] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/shared.tsx:useNamespaceId]

## Invariants

- The pages need `schedule:read`. Creating and editing need `schedule:write` and `run:start`; pausing needs `schedule:operate`; resuming, running now and backfilling need `schedule:operate` and `run:start`. A control the person may not use is not shown, and nothing is asked of the platform before they act. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleControls.tsx:ScheduleControls] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleFormPage.tsx:ScheduleFormPage]
- The Studio never computes a time. The next times, the state, the decisions and the counters are what the platform returned; the form only checks what it can say in words before sending and leaves every rule to the platform. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/SchedulePage.tsx:Upcoming] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:requestOf]
- The input of a schedule is never returned by the platform and is never shown, nor is its digest beyond a short prefix. Editing asks for it again, and what is saved replaces it; a number the browser would round is refused instead of sent. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/SchedulePage.tsx:Summary] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:requestOf]
- Saving never resets what the form does not show (the catch-up window, the buffer limit, the DST rule, an interval offset, a pinned version, the run timeout and the nominal-time field of the action): the draft carries it. A schedule the form cannot write (calendars, exclusions, several rules) or a cron trigger owns is not edited here. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:draftOf] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleFormPage.tsx:Edit]
- An edit saves against the revision it was opened at: the copy of the schedule is never refetched under the person and never kept after the page, and a stale revision is told in words. Deleting is checked against the revision the person saw. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleFormPage.tsx:Edit] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleControls.tsx:DeleteForm]
- A retry of the same request keeps its idempotency key, and a changed request has a new one; a dialog opened again is a new request, so running a schedule twice on purpose is two runs. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/shared.tsx:useCommand]
- The business id of what a schedule starts (feature 018, FR-018-016): a prefix of 1 to 160 visible ASCII characters and two policies, written together or not at all (no prefix: no key, never null or an empty string), shown on the form with the character count, an example of the id (the prefix, a dash and the nominal time in UTC to the second: the next nominal time the platform gave, or the clock when there is none) and one sentence of what the policies do, and on the page of the schedule with the decisions that say, in words, why a held id started nothing. The prefix is customer text: React text only, never in an address, a log or a label other code reads back. The platform decides what is valid; a refusal about the prefix is shown on its field. An empty field is no prefix; spaces alone are refused on the field, never read as "no prefix" and dropped. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:businessPrefixProblem] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:requestOf] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:businessIdPreview] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:businessPolicySentence] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:refusedPrefix] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleFormPage.tsx:Editor] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/SchedulePage.tsx:Summary]
- A state or an outcome is told by a word and a shape as well as a tone; an outcome this version does not know is shown as "another outcome" with its name. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:outcomeView] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/model.ts:scheduleState]

## Side Effects

- The detail page asks the platform for the schedule every 15 seconds, and for the backfills every 3 seconds while one is open; the list and the decisions are read when shown and after an action. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/SchedulePage.tsx:Schedule] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/Backfills.tsx:Backfills]
- The controls post a pause, a resume, a trigger, a backfill or its cancellation, or delete the schedule, announce the result and refresh what they changed; the form posts or puts the schedule and goes to its page. [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleControls.tsx:NoteForm] [frontend/apps/studio/src/features/schedules/ScheduleFormPage.tsx:Editor]
