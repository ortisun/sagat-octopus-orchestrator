[contracts/](contracts/), [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

**Status**: Proposed — no task is started. A task is ticked only with its named evidence (a test and its result,
or a command and its output); a partly done task stays unticked with a dated note. IDs are feature-scoped
(`T019-NNN`, constitution). Tests are written first in each slice and must fail before the implementation.
`[P]` = parallelizable (different files, no dependency on an open task). Story labels use `US0n`. T019-065 and T019-066 were
added after the internal design review and T019-067/068 after the cross-feature review; they sit in their phases by
dependency, so IDs there are not in numeric order.
