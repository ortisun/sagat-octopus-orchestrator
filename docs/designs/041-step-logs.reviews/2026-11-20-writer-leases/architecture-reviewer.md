## Blocker

**B1. A refill timeout can change a step's outcome, which breaks an accepted ADR, and no ADR records the change.** ADR-0097:93-102 and :195-196; ADR-0095:91-94; design 041:1057-1060 and :1190-1192. Past *b*, the report's transaction fails and the worker "resends the report, carry included"; `sdks/SPEC.md:280` retries `CompleteStep`/`FailStep` at most 5 times with the same body. A tenant whose usage row stays hot (near its limit, where design :1238 expects "about one refill per charge") can use up those 5 retries, or outlive its lease while retrying (`STALE_ATTEMPT`): a computed result is lost and the step runs again, possibly duplicating external effects. This breaks ADR-0084 decision 7 (accepted, :172: a refused batch "MUST NOT fail, block, retry or delay a step"), ADR-0095 decision 4 (a carry "MUST NOT change a report's outcome, output or retry") and the design Overview (:24-27: logging only delays a report); neither ADR lists the ADR-0084 clause under "supersedes". Smallest fix: on `tenant_step_log_refill_wait`, the server re-runs the report once itself with the carry downgraded to `MarkOnly` under a new reason (e.g. `quota_busy`), counted and alerted; records are then lost only past *b*, and the report always lands. If the owner prefers failing reports instead, ADR-0097 must supersede ADR-0084 decision 7's clause, state the retry-exhaustion consequence, and T041-36 plus `step-logs-transport-faults` must test the resend and the exhaustion path.

## Major

**M1. *b* is a sizing, not a bound, and its arithmetic is wrong.** Design :1178-1189 and D041-118 (research :129) count one refill holder ahead, but near the limit most charges refill and each refill holds L4 until its report ends; the holder's figure is the fast-path p99 (*r* = 32.7 ms) where a refill is the slow path (≈ 95 ms p99, ADR-0097:225, D041-117); purge deletes (up to 500 rows) and limit changes also hold L4 and are not in the formula; *h* was measured at 1 × 16 only (D041-106). `lock_timeout` applies per acquisition: a queued row lock is a tuple lock then an xid wait (and a re-lock after the row is updated), so one statement can wait ≈ 2*b*. Arithmetic: "`max_connections` up to 1,103 at 5 s" (design :1189) ignores the rounding to whole seconds; with *b* rounded and required below 5 s the maximum is 881 (880 × 4.5 + 37.2 = 3,997.2 ms → 4 s; 882 gives 4,001.7 ms → 5 s); under the 2*b* reading it is 437. Fix: call *b* a sizing; clamp it inside `sagat_quota_step_log_refill` to `(current_setting('statement_timeout') − margin) / 2`; correct 1,103 (ADR-0097:96-100, design :1186-1189, D041-118).

**M2. The startup check is a phrase, not a task.** Design :1188 assigns it to T041-5, but T041-5 (:2275-2305) has no such step, file in an app crate or test. Startup is the wrong moment: `max_connections` changes with a failover to a new primary, and `statement_timeout` is lowered per transaction (`crates/infra-postgres/src/instances.rs:120-122` sets 3 s). The prototype test `the_refill_bound_follows_max_connections` (step_log_leases_tests.rs:1535-1562) re-implements the formula in Rust and never relates *b* to the timeout; it was "seen failing" only because the function did not exist. Fix: the runtime clamp of M1 plus a named test such as `the_refill_bound_stays_under_the_transactions_statement_timeout`.

**M3. The error mapping contradicts itself and does not fit the error model.** data-model.md:471 maps every error of the carry call through `store_error_without_text` (SQLSTATE only) to `StepLogCarry`, whose re-run carries nothing — so the refill timeout would be downgraded and its records dropped; data-model.md:541-542 says the opposite; design :1074-1075 says the same as :471. `StoreError::Unavailable` has only a free-text `detail` (`identity.rs:746`); `sdks/SPEC.md:63` carries a reason only as the `x-sagat-error` value, existing reasons are uppercase (`TOKEN_PROVIDER_FAILED`), and `step_log_quota_busy` is in neither `contracts.md` nor `sdks/error-catalog.json`. A statement timeout during the wait (57014, possible per M1) has no mapping and would fall into `StepLogCarry`. Fix: a typed `StoreError::StepLogQuotaBusy` matched by constraint name before the catch-all; gRPC `UNAVAILABLE` with `x-sagat-error: STEP_LOG_QUOTA_BUSY` added to the contract and catalog; named tests in T041-36 for the store mapping and the API answer.

**M4. The refill-timeout alert will page on normal ingest backpressure.** data-model.md:248-253 advances the same sequence when a refill passes the caller's lower `lock_timeout`, the ingest's 200 ms included (1,075 such timeouts were seen at 8 × 128); ADR-0097:135 alerts "at any increase over 5 minutes". Sequences can jump by up to 32 after crash recovery, so a failover looks like timeouts. Section 9 (design :1686-1692) lists no such alert and no task for `alerts.yaml`. Fix: separate carry and ingest counts (two sequences or a closed `caller` label) and alert only on the carry; name the alert and its task; consider counting in-process at the store mapping of M3, which is exact.

**M5. The grant rule differs between the ADR and what was built and measured.** ADR-0097 decision 4 (:108-111), design :1175 and D041-117 fold only `used`, keep the room and grant max(*n* − room, …); data-model.md:255-257, the prototype `step_log_spike.rs:361-367` and the fourth-run declaration (design :2161) fold the whole lease and grant max(*n*, …); mutation W8 (validation-report :785) tests the prototype's form. Fix: adopt the measured form in ADR-0097, design 2b and a correction note to D041-117.

**M6. The held-room bound is misstated, its derivation absent, and it is pinned at a single point.** ADR-0097:114, design :1209 and G041-023 (research :157): "(≥ L / √(π*k*))" is false — C(2*k*, *k*)/4^*k* < 1/√(π*k*) for every *k* ≥ 1 (at *k* = 8 the claimed lower bound is ≈ 209,160 > the true 205,920). The bound uses *L* where it needs F₀: with usage S > 0 it is false (at *k* = 1 with S = L/2, L/4 remains, not L/2). The derivation (each grant ≤ F/(2(A+1)) with A ≥ i − 1, so F_i ≥ F_{i−1}(1 − 1/(2i)), and Π (2i−1)/(2i) = C(2*k*,*k*)/4^*k*; the 1 MiB cap only lowers grants) appears only in a test doc comment (step_log_leases_tests.rs:394-396). `room_held_by_open_writers_is_bounded_by_the_grant` pins only *k* = 8 from zero usage, and no recorded mutation enlarges grants (W2 shrinks them). Fix: state the derivation in three lines in ADR decision 4 and design 2b; correct to F₀·C(2*k*,*k*)/4^*k* ≥ F₀/(2√*k*); parametrise the test over *k* ∈ {1, 2, 8, 32} with a nonzero starting S; record a mutation with grant F/(A + 1).

**M7. The lock-order fitness function contradicts the design, and the fitness row enforces the rejected decision.** Design :885-886 says the test "checks that after L4 … no writer takes a lock on an existing row", but the refill locks its own existing lease `FOR UPDATE` after L4 (ADR-0097:109-110; data-model.md:287-289 says so; prototype :355-356). D041-117 (research :128) states yet another order (fence → lease → usage row). `architecture-principles.md:31` still says "a carry's refill takes the row `NOWAIT`". Fix: take the own lease `FOR UPDATE NOWAIT` after L4 — only an L4 holder can hold it, so this never fails short of a defect or a prepared transaction, and it makes "nothing waits after L4" mechanically true; model the bounded L4 wait under L0 in `lock_order.rs`; rewrite row 31 (refill waits at most *b*; nothing waits after L4; no transaction waits for a lease).

**M8. Waiting for the usage row under the run lock goes against a force the ADR itself states.** ADR-0097:60: "a report must never wait under its run lock for another … replica's or attempt's commit"; decision 4 does exactly that for up to *b*. Nothing states the consequence: sibling reports, heartbeats (which hold the run row, ADR-0095 option 3), cancels and signals of the same run queue behind that wait; no Risks row covers it, and :1739 still says the report "commits in its first transaction". Fix: restate the force as "bounded by *b*"; add the consequence and a Risks row; have T041-9 measure the run-row wait for a fan-out run near the limit.

**M9. The fourth run cannot fail on the failure mode it introduces.** Design :2166-2167 makes "a refill past *b*" void a round, and void jobs are re-run, so that failure only ever voids rounds and leaves the run "not decided", like the third run. D041-118 says "the protocol unchanged"; the declaration says "everything else is the third run's" while adding this voiding clause and the per-path counts. It names the develop merge (`99992c70` via `5458820b`) but not the commit measured, and its "expected" figures compare against the second and third runs' medians, taken on a different build (tonic 0.14.9 and others). Fix: make any refill timeout a gate failure (or set a threshold); name the measured commit when the run is pushed; list exactly which rules change from the third run.

## Minor

- **Stale shared-pool, `NOWAIT` and old-figure text:** `docs/adr/README.md:39` ("up to 256 … leases taken `SKIP LOCKED`"); ADR-0095:21-22 ("answering at once"), :131 ("third run"), :142-144 ("one subtransaction", "waited for at most 1 s"); ADR-0027:201 and ADR-0084:254 ("one of the tenant's … leases"); design :25 ("once per lease"), :874 (the purge takes L3b; it takes no lease), :896-897 (the L4 holders omit carrying reports' refills), :1050 ("one of the tenant's leases taken `SKIP LOCKED`"), :1111 (23 nested statements, against 26 in 2b :1251 and data-model :305), :1737 ("third run"), :1754 ("at most 256 lease rows"), :1782 (a held usage row now means a wait, not `contended`), :1793 ("three metrics"); G041-023 (research :157: "1/64 of the free room"); D041-117 (research :128: "lease rows per tenant", against the no-tenant labels).
- **Decision 7 is not wired into the code that will read usage.** data-model §6 (:363-364) and the code already built (`crates/infra-postgres/src/quotas.rs:1228-1235`) read S directly; decision 7 requires S + Σ `used` through `sagat_quota_step_log_usage`. Add `quotas.rs` and a test to T041-5.
- **The acceptance condition points at the wrong run.** ADR-0097:8 and design :431 (O041-040) wait for "the third run recorded", which already exists and measured the rejected pool. Point them at the fourth run, and register an O-question for accepting ADR-0097.
- **Subtransaction count.** The refill's own `EXCEPTION` block (data-model.md:252) means a carry without a reservation opens two nested subtransactions, and an ingest refill one; ADR-0097:197-199 and design :1755 say one.
- **The test GUC can raise the bound.** In the prototype `sagat.step_log_refill_wait` replaces *b* rather than lowering it (step_log_spike.rs:324-327; tests set 60 s); `'0'` means no lock timeout at all. Design :2299-2300 says it "can only lower". Use `least()`, or read no session GUC in a definer function.
- **"About one refill in 300 far from the limit" has a hidden condition:** it needs F ≥ 2(A + 1) MiB, and A counts zero-room leases; with ≈ 160 backends that is 320 MiB of the 1 GiB default. State it.
- **Replica attribution does not work.** Every replica gets the same `application_name` (`pool.rs:70`), so design :1244-1245's claim is false; pids from an old primary mean nothing after failover, and a pooler shows its last client. Record the instance id in the lease row or drop the claim; option 1c's fallback ("if backend keys prove hard to observe") needs a criterion.
- **The "cannot deadlock" argument omits L4 holders.** `take_log_prefix` (`content_usage.rs:256`; callers in scripts, dispatch and connectors) takes L4 early and then writes more rows (`scripts.rs:1855-1886`, `dispatch.rs:251-260`). I found no cycle with what a refill waiter holds, but the argument and the lock-order test's writer set should include them.
- **The down migration is one-way once lines exist** (it refuses while any line exists, so rolling back after use means purging every line). Say so in ADR-0097's Consequences.
