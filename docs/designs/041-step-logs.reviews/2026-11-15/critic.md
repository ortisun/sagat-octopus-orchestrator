## Major

1. The completion-wait bound is wrong (design-1-1). FR-041-008 (lines 136-143), Goals (255-256), Open Questions (283-287), Permits (455-457), step 7 (492-499), line 511-512, Risks (745), Alternatives (798-800), SC-041-001 (216-220), SC-041-007 (236-240). Row-lock waiters queue first come first served, so a completion waits for every ingest commit queued ahead of it (the per-tenant sub-cap of 4 is per replica: 8 replicas give 32). The quota flush is a deferred `FOR EACH ROW` constraint trigger (`crates/infra-postgres/migrations/20261024000000_durable_quotas.up.sql:446-558`): a 500-row insert queues 500 `sagat_quota_flush_deltas()` calls, the first takes the tenant row (`:348`) and the rest run while it is held, and each row upserts the shared `quota_transaction_deltas` row (`:177-187`). SC-041-001 holds the ingest before the finalizer runs, and SC-041-007 uses ten lines per step, so neither can fail on this. *Recommendation:* state the bound as queue depth × commit hold (which grows with rows per batch); make the per-tenant cap global or state the multiplication; add a T041-6 scenario with full batches from many attempts and completions alongside; re-weigh chunk rows against the per-row flush cost; consider one batch per process.
2. A tenant at its limit stays on the locking path for days (design-1-2). FR-041-008 (139-141), step 7, FR-041-009 (144-147), Risks (745). Log bytes fall only at purge (14 days) and `tenant_log_bytes` is shared with script logs, so every batch, final flushes included, takes both fences and the tenant row even when nothing can be stored. *Recommendation:* when the unlocked read is at or over the limit, store nothing, record `tenant_quota` on the stream and answer `STOP_ATTEMPT`; add the near-limit and at-limit paths to T041-6 and the Risks table.
3. Two hot rows per batch are missing from the risks (design-1-3). Step 2 (473-477), step 7 (492-493), lock order (514-516), CUJ 6 (369-374). `run_log_usage FOR UPDATE` is held from step 7 to after commit (and near the limit across a 200 ms wait), so a 200-iteration `forEach` puts about 200 batches/s on one row; the membership row is locked `FOR SHARE` per batch (`sagat_lock_memberships`, `20261126000000_authority_read_locks.up.sql:7-9`), creating MultiXacts at about one per logging attempt per second. *Recommendation:* enforce the run ceiling without a row lock or take it last; add fan-out and per-worker concurrency scenarios to T041-6 and T041-9; add both rows to the Risks table.
4. Stream and usage rows are kept forever, and the purge index cannot bound a pass (design-1-4). FR-041-009, section 3 (526-537), section 4 (566-571), NFR-041-COST (824-825), Non-Goals (270-271). One `protected_system` row per logging attempt and per run, never deleted, uncharged, with no growth estimate; with retention 1-365 days per namespace a partial index on `last_at` makes each pass rescan every unpurged stream older than the shortest retention. *Recommendation:* a retention for stream and usage rows; a growth estimate; purge per namespace or store `expires_at`.
5. Rows that carry few or no bytes are not bounded by the tenant quota (design-1-5). Section 3 Accounting (546-551), FR-041-006, NFR-041-COST, Constitution Check V (765). `sagat_quota_measure` charges only `message` and `fields` (`durable_quotas.up.sql:152-173`), so an empty line or a marker costs nothing, while a row costs perhaps 150-250 bytes; only the per-run ceiling bounds the count. *Recommendation:* charge a fixed per-record overhead or add a tenant record quota, or have the owner accept the gap in writing (quota bypass is release-blocking under the constitution).
6. The classified-namespace flag can stay unset for good (design-1-6). FR-041-021 (201-204), FR-041-013 (164-167), section 3 (538, 553-555), section 10 (731-738). Old API replicas keep publishing during a rolling upgrade after the backfill and never set the flag; since it is never cleared or re-evaluated, logs of a classified namespace are then readable without the PII grant (OD-2026-11-13-18), silently. `UPDATE (has_classified_schemas)` for `sagat_app` allows a true-to-false change. *Recommendation:* set it by a database trigger on the version insert, or reconcile after rollout, or compute it at read time; refuse clearing it in the database; an upgrade test with an old binary publishing after the migration.
7. The read horizon couples tenants and has no lag signal (design-1-7). Section 5 (587-597), FR-041-012 (159-163), Risks (749), section 9 (708-721). Any long or idle-in-transaction or prepared transaction freezes new lines and keeps every tail from ending, for every tenant; no metric, alert, PostgreSQL setting or bound on the time to `end`. *Recommendation:* a horizon-lag metric and alert; state the required settings; a bounded "may be incomplete" `end`; a Risks row.
8. The Goals, the first open question and the Overview contradict FR-041-008 (structural-1-3, style-1-1 first part). Goals (255-256) and Open Questions (283) say the tenant row is taken only at commit, and the Overview (18-19) says a log is never "a reason for a completion to wait"; FR-041-008, step 7, Risks (745) and ADR-0084 decision 3 take it before the insert within 2 MiB of the limit. *Recommendation:* restate both with the near-limit case and cite FR-041-008 and SC-041-001/007 rather than paraphrase.
9. The secret claims are stated more absolutely than the design supports (style-1-1 second part). Goals (258), CUJ 1 (335), Constitution Check V (765) say a delivered secret "is not stored" or "shows as `[REDACTED]`"; FR-041-007 redacts only verbatim values of at least 6 bytes and G041-001 accepts the rest. *Recommendation:* limit each claim and cite G041-001.
