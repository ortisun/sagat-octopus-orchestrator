## Major

1. Merge the tenant-allowance recommendations into one rule with no locking path (raises stage 5 #6 from Nit to Major and changes its fix; concerns critic #2 and critic #8). FR-041-008 (136-143), Goals (255-256), Open Questions (283-287), step 7 (492-499), lock order (514-519), Complexity Tracking row 4 (778), ADR-0084 decision 3.
   Together the entries keep an unlocked read, a 2 MiB band and a locking retry, and critic #2 adds a fourth behaviour at the limit. The locking path exists to "keep the records that fit", a rule ADR-0027 (lines 75-77) states to protect terminal reports; a step-log batch is lossy (ADR-0084 decision 7), and dropping one counted batch of at most 192 KiB at a 1 GiB limit loses nothing promised. The locking path is what makes the Goals and Overview false (critic #8), what holds `run_log_usage` across a 200 ms tenant wait (critic #3), and why the fences sit in the lock order.
   *Recommendation:* the ingest never takes the tenant row before commit; when the unlocked read is over the limit, or the finalizer raises `tenant_log_quota_exceeded`, the batch stores nothing, the stream records `tenant_quota` with the count, and the verdict of entry 3 is returned; remove the band, the locking path and `sagat_quota_lock_log_usage` from the ingest; rewrite ADR-0084 decision 3 in the T041-2 revision and say that ADR-0027's prefix rule applies to terminal reports.
2. The fallback order contradicts the per-row trigger mechanics, and the spike runs after the schema is fixed (goes beyond critic #1; rejects part of its fix). Open Questions (283-287), Alternatives (788-790, 798-800), T041-5, T041-6, T041-9, Estimate (1046-1047), ADR-0084 Consequences.
   The first flush call takes the tenant row (`durable_quotas.up.sql:348`) and each of the other calls still runs its reads and `DELETE` while it is held (`:302-304`, `:368-369`), so the hold grows with rows per transaction. A grouped commit, the first fallback, lengthens each hold while cutting their number; chunk rows cut both about tenfold and bound the uncharged rows of critic #5, but the spike that would choose them runs in T041-6, after T041-5 merged the one-row-per-line migration: a dead end built in by task order.
   *Recommendation:* the T041-6 spike compares one row per line with chunk rows under critic #1's load (full batches, many attempts, completions alongside), on a scratch migration before T041-5 merges; drop the grouped commit as the named first fallback unless the spike shows it helps; a result that overturns O041-004 goes to the owner. Of critic #1's remedies keep "queue depth × commit hold", reject "a global per-tenant cap" (a new cross-replica hot spot) and "one batch per process" (breaks FR-041-004's per-attempt fence) unless the spike shows the need.
3. `STOP_ATTEMPT` means two things, so SC-041-002 and CUJ 3 cannot hold; stage 5 #3's fix depends on resolving this (new). Section 1 verdicts (419-422), section 6 (646-649), FR-041-004, FR-041-006, step 9 (504-507), SC-041-002 (221-223), CUJ 3 (345-351), FR-041-017 (187-188, 702).
   The verdict is sent at a ceiling and after `final`; the SDK stops sending in both cases, so after a ceiling no final batch arrives, `final_at` stays empty, every flooded attempt shows "ended without its final flush", the server never learns the later drops, and the reason `STOPPED_BY_SERVER` (line 417) can never be sent. Stage 5 #3 (highest sequence on the final batch) needs the final batch to still be sent.
   *Recommendation:* the ceiling verdict means "send no more lines, but send the final batch, empty, with `final = true` and the highest assigned sequence"; keep "send nothing" for a recorded `final` and a deleted stream; then adopt stage 5 #3, with its unlisted edits: CUJ 3 and FR-041-011 can only say "dropped by the worker" or "by the server", the `where="sdk"` reasons of `step_log_lines_dropped_total` (line 716) move to an SDK-local metric, and ADR-0084 decision 7 changes in T041-2.
