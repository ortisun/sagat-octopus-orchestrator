## Major

1. r1-M3 (partly answered): the near-limit wait bound and the "one batch's commit" bound are wrong. "Near the limit the bound is the 200 ms ingest lock timeout" (1011; Risks 745; ADR-0084 106-107): `lock_timeout` bounds how long the ingest waits, not how long it holds the row; the near-limit path holds the tenant row across a 500-row insert (500 trigger calls) and the commit, and a completion sets no `lock_timeout` (only the pool's 5 s `statement_timeout`, `pool.rs:33`). "One log batch's commit" (141-142, Goals 255-256) is wrong too: the completion queues behind every ingest commit ahead of it, up to the per-tenant sub-cap × replicas (4 × R). *Fix:* `SET LOCAL statement_timeout` on the near-limit path; restate the bound as k ≤ 4 × replicas commits; a T041-6 near-limit barrier test measuring the completion's wait.
2. r1-M5 (partly answered): step 9 (505) assigns `last_sequence` the batch's highest sequence instead of a maximum. Late batches B0 (1..100) and B1 (1..256) still in flight after the SDK gave up on them; B2 (257..512 plus DROPPED[1..256]) commits first; B1 then sets `last_sequence = 256` and B0 sets 100; a resend of B2 counts DROPPED 101..256 again, so `dropped_by_sdk` is 412 for 256 lost lines; step 5's duplicate removal also needs `last_sequence` never to fall. *Fix:* `last_sequence = GREATEST(last_sequence, max(batch))`; a T041-8 case for a late batch after a newer one.
3. M1: step logs fill the shared `tenant_log_bytes`, and every other log writer is then stored empty (confirmed in code). FR-041-006 (117), O041-005 (310-311), step 7 (496-497). `take_log_prefix` (`content_usage.rs:245-274`) cuts text to what is left under the limit; it writes `inbox_events.last_error` (`dispatch.rs:219`), `event_connectors.last_error` (`connectors.rs:626`) and the script build log, failure text, test runs and executions (`scripts.rs:1161, 1328, 1413, 1490, 1668, 1855`). A tenant whose steps log a lot fills usage to the limit until the purge (14 days), and every dispatch retry error, connector error and script build failure is stored as `''` with `truncated = true`. The design and the owner question name only script logs (46-47, 310-311). *Fix:* stop step logs at the limit minus a reserve, or a separate resource (an ADR-0027 amendment); put the consequence to the owner; the test "tenant at its limit through step logs; a connector error is still stored".
4. M2: completions also wait behind ingest through the namespace secrets lock queue (confirmed in code). Step 2 (473-477), lock order (514-516), FR-041-008, SC-041-001. `current_worker_authorized` holds `share_secrets_lock` to commit (`execution.rs:1017-1027`, `quotas.rs:307-321`); exclusive takers include `lock_secrets` in `execution/database.rs:96, 113, 364, 609`, `secrets.rs:368, 480`, `managed_secrets.rs:120, 235` and `secret_cleanup.rs`; a shared request queued behind a waiting exclusive one waits. An ingest in namespace N holds the shared lock while it inserts; a database step asks exclusively and waits; a completion of another run in N, holding its run row, asks shared and queues behind it, so that run's transitions wait on the ingest. *Fix:* move the authority block to just before the insert (no path takes the secrets lock after a stream row); a barrier test with a `lock_secrets` waiter; state the wait in FR-041-008 and ADR-0084 decision 3.
5. M3: the session check breaks on reconnect, and skips expiry and namespace (token replacement confirmed in code). Step 2 (473-474), section 1 (411-412, 419-421), section 6 (646-647). Re-registering with the same (tenant, subject, worker_name) overwrites `token_hash` (`workers.rs:338-343`) while leases survive; after an API restart (named by T041-8) the running attempt's sender still sends the old token, gets `STOP_CAPTURE`, treats it as a session failure, and capture stops for every in-flight attempt with a valid lease. Step 2 checks neither `expires_at` nor the session's namespace. *Fix:* the SDK reads the current token at each send; a token mismatch on a live lease answers `RETRY_LATER` or a `SESSION_REPLACED` verdict; check expiry and namespace; a conformance case that re-registers mid-attempt.
6. M4: the flood accounting cannot meet SC-041-002 or CUJ 3 (confirmed in the text). SC-041-002 (221-223), CUJ 3 (349-351), FR-041-006 (117-118) against step 6 (489-490) and `STOP_ATTEMPT` (420, 646). With 1,000,000 lines, about 990,000 are dropped before the 10,000-record ceiling but `dropped_by_sdk` is clamped at 10,000; after the ceiling nothing more is sent, so later drops are counted nowhere and `STOPPED_BY_SERVER` can never be delivered; `step-logs-limits` will fail or be blessed with wrong numbers. *Fix:* saturate at the `DROPPED` record's own sequence minus `last_sequence` (cap 2^53), not at the ceiling; accept one count-only `final` batch after `STOP_ATTEMPT` (closes r1-N7); otherwise reword SC-041-002.
7. M5 (plausible): a purge can race a late batch and leave rows nothing deletes. Section 4 (566-571), step 4 (484-486), purge index (531). Retention 1 day, a step logs at its start, runs 30 hours, sends its final batch B; B reads the live lease; the completion commits; the purge finds the stream expired, locks it before B reaches step 4, deletes and sets `purged_at`; B inserts. The index (`last_at WHERE purged_at IS NULL`) never selects the stream again; the rows and their bytes stay forever and readers are told "expired". *Fix:* step 4 stores nothing and answers `STOP_ATTEMPT` when `purged_at` or `deleted_at` is set; a T041-10 test with a batch held between steps 3 and 4.
8. M6 (plausible): a script marker can commit after the tail sent `end`. FR-041-012 (162-163), section 5 (594-597), section 7 (674-681). `record_execution` (`scripts.rs:1775-1903`) reads the step without checking the lease or run state and does not take its xid first; the runner records "even if the lease is lost" (`apps/sandbox-runner/src/lib.rs:928-931, 951`) and retries (`:1075-1083`); if the run is cancelled while it records, the tail ends first. *Fix:* take the xid first and insert the marker only when `runs.finished_at IS NULL` in the same read, or state the exception in FR-041-012.
9. M7 (plausible): a secret leaves the redaction set when its attempt ends. Section 6 (638-640), FR-041-007 (124-125), ADR-0085 decision 1 (46-47) and option 4 (87-88). A resolves S, caches a client in a module-level variable, ends; S leaves the set; B logs the client and S is stored. SC-041-004 and `step-logs-redaction` test only concurrent attempts. *Fix:* keep values for the life of the process (bounded, documented), or until every attempt that overlapped A has ended; a one-after-the-other case.
10. M8 (plausible): `run_log_usage` makes all batches of a run wait for each other, including during the commit's wait for the tenant row (`durable_quotas.up.sql:345-348`). Step 7 (492-493), lock order (515-516). A `forEach` of 200 items run 64 at a time puts about 64 batches/s on one row; under 200 ms they get `RETRY_LATER`, and after two retries the SDK drops them as `SEND_FAILED` on a healthy system; SC-041-007 measures ten lines per step. *Fix:* the wide-run case in the T041-6 spike and T041-9; make the run-usage update the last statement (`UPDATE … WHERE records + n <= ceiling RETURNING`, trimming on failure), or state the serialisation in FR-041-008.

## Nit

11. r1-N7 (partly answered): see the table and M4.

13. N1: read order is transaction-id order, not commit order (FR-041-002 97-98 against section 5 587); say "the order of the storing transaction's id, then row id".
14. N2: "the resend gets the same answer" (step 10 508-509, Verification 1013) is false for a `final` batch, whose resend gets `STOP_ATTEMPT`; require that nothing is stored and the verdict is not an error.
15. N3: the duplicate-batch barrier test (1014) holds the first batch while the second waits under a 200 ms lock timeout, so it can only produce `RETRY_LATER`; release on the waiter appearing in `pg_locks`, or assert `RETRY_LATER` then a resend that stores nothing.
16. N4: `STOP_CAPTURE` has no reason (420-421), so "when it was a session failure, for the session" (646-647) cannot be implemented; add a reason enumeration (capture off, lease, authority, session).
17. N5: a `DROPPED` record's own sequence is undefined (FR-041-002 94-96 against 462): sharing the worker counter makes a lost marker count as a dropped line; not sharing it can collide on the primary key and be discarded by `DO NOTHING`.
18. N6: script stream identity: NULLs are distinct in the unique key (530-532) so `ON CONFLICT` never matches a script stream (gate on `inserted`, `scripts.rs:1889`, or `NULLS NOT DISTINCT`); the generation comes from the latest `step.leased` (`scripts.rs:1798-1802`), which after a reclaim is the newer holder's.
19. N7: the first batch of a run has no `run_log_usage` row (step 7 493); use `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … RETURNING`, as step 4 does.
20. N8: "at most once per 300 s" (FR-041-013 169-170, section 5 603-606) has no mechanism, and the Logs tab calls the streams, list and tail routes together (689-699), so two first reads race; a throttle row keyed (tenant, subject, run) with a conditional upsert.
21. N9: the runner check uses the token's role only (459-460); `current_worker_authorized` refuses only database-runner (`execution.rs:1040-1043`); also refuse `Role::Runner` in the membership read.
