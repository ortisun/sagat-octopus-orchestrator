1. **Blocker. Requirement 013-016, Technical Plan 2 (third architecture rule), security Major 5's fix.** No single store method reads memberships for authority today: about twelve transactional re-checks call `public.sagat_lock_memberships` (`FOR SHARE`, migration `20261126000000_authority_read_locks`) from `secrets.rs`, `managed_secrets.rs`, `workers.rs`, `schedules.rs`, `reveal_grants.rs`, `payload_disclosure.rs`, `event_payloads.rs`, `execution.rs`, `execution/database.rs`, `execution/resets.rs`, `database.rs` and `database/admission.rs`; three plain reads exist (`identity.rs`, `approval_progress.rs`, `declared_operations.rs`). The requirement's list omits secret disclosure to workers, database admission, reveal grants, payload disclosure, approvals, resets and declared operations; the planned rule would fail on today's tree or be weakened. Fix: in the 013 migration redefine `sagat_lock_memberships` with `AND m.status = 'ACTIVE'` (restored by `down`); change the three plain reads; the architecture rule refuses `FROM/JOIN memberships` outside `infra-postgres/src/identity.rs` and migrations, with a negative fixture; one integration test per caller family.

2. **Blocker. Requirement 013-032, Rollout, Constitution Check IV.** Federation is switched on per process and advertised as advice, but federated-only, suspension and the `user:` refusal must hold on every control-plane and engine process; an older or federation-off replica would accept what the tenant refused. Architecture decision 0050 decision 9 requires such a feature to be activated through the instance-capability registry (as Architecture decision 0064 decision 7 did for 029), and no record states the divergence. Fix: a capability `identity-federation-v1` listed only when a process's configuration enables federation, for the control plane and the engine; an audited operator activation under Architecture decision 0082 decision 1; refuse connection creation, SCIM, suspension and federated-only until it is recorded; derive design 050's names from the requirement; binary rollback below the requirement unsupported (Architecture decision 0140); an older-replica test.

3. **Major. Technical Plan 6, Requirement 013-016, the group-source contract.** Only just-in-time provisioning cites slot 4a. A SCIM deactivation writes `memberships` and then 027 app-session rows (slot 1, locked `FOR UPDATE` by admissions before `memberships FOR SHARE`): a cycle. SCIM and assertion deliveries take the exclusive 029 tenant access fence and then change the role, the reverse of admissions' slot 4a order; assertion storage at every redemption would serialize a tenant's sign-ins against its sensitive decisions. Fix: the slot sequence of each write transaction (redemption, SCIM user, SCIM group, role change, connection disable, raised context); do not write app sessions (they refuse a suspended member on each call); memberships before the fence or a recorded argued deviation; add the paths to `slot_order.rs` and 029's `lock_order.rs`.

4. **Major. Decision 013-003, Technical Plan 4 (Revocation set), Architecture decision 0140 option 4.** Every request already reads the membership, so joining `signin_sessions` by `sid` into that statement gives immediate revocation with no new round trip and removes the poller, the fail-closed mode, Gap 013-003, the revocation table and the window argument; it was not considered. As designed, the poll's connection pool, the set's size bound, a staleness metric and an alert are missing. Fix: evaluate the join in Architecture decision 0140; if the poll is kept, give it its own connection, a row ceiling with a fail mode, a gauge and an alert.

5. **Major. Requirement 013-025, Decision 013-015, Technical Plans 2 and 9, Rollout step 2.** No key lifecycle: where the active key version is recorded so replicas agree, and that a new public key reaches every replica before anyone signs with it (a Transit rotate signs at once); "the control plane creates its signing key" races between replicas and gives the serving process an act Architecture decision 0082 decision 1 makes the operator's; with Transit every renewal is an OpenBao call and an outage signs everyone out within five minutes, unstated and unalerted; the signer duplicates the OpenBao client of `crates/infra-secrets/src/openbao.rs`; `SecretProvider` is namespace-keyed, and no port writes connection secrets; the two-system write does not follow Architecture decision 0035 decision 7 (intent, write, verify, activate); OpenBao exists only in the `compose.database.yaml` overlay, so "compose gains nothing" and "every flow on a laptop" are untrue. Fix: the lifecycle in Architecture decision 0140 (an operator command creates and rotates; the active version stored in PostgreSQL; publish, wait one key-set refresh, then sign); state the OpenBao dependency, alert and degradation; `TokenSigner` and an identity-secret port in `infra-secrets`; the Architecture decision 0035 decision 7 protocol for client secrets; the bootstrap in the development overlay and its runbook.

6. **Major. Requirement 013-025, Technical Plans 3 and 6, the tasks.** The design takes work owned by others without approved dependencies: feature 015 ("OpenBao as the secret and key authority", Architecture decision 0035 decision 8 leaves platform signing-key migration to it) has no design, yet 013 decides the mount layout, policies, Transit and signing keys; it writes 027's app-session table, creates the external-group tables 029 builds on, and names tests against features 026 and 027, which are not built; no task carries the approved-dependency line. Fix: record in 015's roadmap row that 013 owns only the identity mount and the signing key for 0.3.0 behind a port 015 may re-home; `Owner-gate:` or dependency lines on the tasks that touch 026, 027 and 029.

7. **Major. Technical Plans 2 and 4.** The execution engine verifies bearer tokens through the same `Core`, `authenticate_bearer` and `IdentityService` (`crates/api-grpc/src/service.rs`, `apps/execution-engine/src/lib.rs`) and accepts only the deployment issuer, so the federated-only check also runs there and a federated developer in a federated-only tenant cannot run `sagat worker dev` or a local SDK worker; `Core::verifier()` returns the concrete verifier and `Core::authenticate(tenant, subject)` carries no issuer or `jti` (`crates/api-http/src/core.rs`). Fix: state whether the engine accepts platform tokens (then it needs the revocation check and the registry capability) or how a federated developer runs a worker (a machine member bound by an administrator) and record the gap; widen `Core::authenticate` to take the issuer kind, `jti` and `sid`.

8. **Major. Technical Plan 3 (external groups), 029 `data-model.md`, `contracts/evaluation.md`.** 029 expects a source to add columns to `access_groups` with members in `access_group_members`, and its foreign keys (`role_assignments.group_id`, `folder_entries`) point there; separate tables owned by 013 cannot be named by an assignment, so CUJ 4 cannot be built; no rule for deleting a connection whose groups are named; Task 029-057 writes rows into an unowned schema. Fix: one schema (013 adds source, connection, external value, generation and freshness columns to `access_groups`, or 029 amends its keys); which migration lands first; the connection-delete rule; recorded in both documents.

9. **Major. Requirement 013-005, Decision 013-004, Requirement 027-014, Architecture decision 0062 decision 2.** Requirement 027-014 still redeems at the configured issuer and verifies the token; design 013 redeems in process but defines no port, return type or owner; app sessions expire "no later than the exchanged token", so with 300-second tokens embedded apps reopen the popup every five minutes; revoking a sign-in session does not end its app sessions. Fix: a `PlatformSignIn::redeem_for_app` port returning subject, tenant and `sid`; app session lifetime and revocation bound to the sign-in session; amend Requirement 027-014 in the same delivery.

10. **Major. Architecture decision 0140 "Refines", Technical Plan 7, `SAGAT_PUBLIC_ORIGIN`, Architecture decision 0062 decision 8.** The platform issuer and its anonymous sign-in pages add an origin setting and pages that decision 8 forbids on the Studio origin; nothing requires `SAGAT_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` to equal the Studio origin (needed by `__Host-` and same-origin renewal) and differ from the apps, sandbox and public-forms origins, or refuses `/v1/auth/*` on the apps origin. Fix: refine decision 8 with the row, the start-up equality refusals and the exception for platform-authored sign-in pages; a start-up test.

11. **Major. Technical Plans 7 and 10, Rollout step 2.** `config.json` carries one `oidc` object from which `connect-src` is derived (`crates/api-http/src/studio.rs`), so naming the platform issuer drops the deployment issuer's origin while the sign-in page still offers it; `finishLogin` redeems with `credentials: 'omit'` (`oidc.ts`), so the browser discards the cookie set at redemption. Fix: a `config.json` shape with both issuers and a contract test; keep the deployment issuer in `connect-src` while offered; `credentials: 'same-origin'` for redemption and renewal; correct the claim.

12. **Major. Architecture decision 0140 decision 3, Technical Plan 4, Non-functional requirement 013-004.** Refusing deployment-issuer subjects starting `user:` breaks existing deployments: the platform's own contract examples use that form (`tests/contract/payload-classification/openapi.rs`, `user:operator@example.test`). Fix: a prefix in a reserved namespace enforced by `Subject::parse` like `system:`, or a migration that refuses with a named error while a membership already uses it.

13. **Major. Technical Plan 3, Requirement 013-032, Rollback.** A member can be suspended without any connection, so a `down` that refuses only while a connection exists drops `memberships.status` and reactivates them; "disable federation" silently removes federated-only and locks out every `user:` member. Fix: `down` refuses while a membership is `SUSPENDED` or any federation row exists; the rollback states what happens to federated-only tenants and federated members, with a runbook step.

14. **Major. Technical Plan 6 (Role mapping), Requirement 013-022, the purges, the stale-SCIM alert.** An assertion-mapped role is lowered by a later leased pass, so between expiry and the pass other sessions act with the higher role (fail open); the pass, the domain re-check, the purges and the alert have no process, lease kind or registry capability (Architecture decision 0082 decision 7). Fix: compute the effective role in the per-request membership read (the default role once an assertion source's expiry passed), the pass only tidies and audits; place every pass in the placement table with its lease and capability.

15. **Major. Technical Plan 9, Architecture decision 0140 "Refines", `apps/dev-idp`, `apps/cli/src/dev.rs`.** Architecture decision 0019 decision 3 makes the development provider accept `application/json` only, at most 4 KiB, its defence against cross-site form posts; the design adds browser navigation, `/saml/sso` and a form-encoded `/token` on the path `sagat dev token` posts JSON to, and Architecture decision 0140 says it refines decision 3 without saying how. Fix: separate paths for the new flows, keep `/token` JSON, and a numbered decision in Architecture decision 0140 stating which protections hold for the browser routes.

16. **Major. 17. **Major. 18. **Major.
