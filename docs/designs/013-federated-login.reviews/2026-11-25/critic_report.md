Counts: Blocker 0, Major 9, Nit 9

1. **Major. Requirements 013-001, 013-003, 013-021; Terms.** Any change of endpoints, client authentication or mapping returns a connection to `DRAFT` and revokes its sessions, so rotating an OIDC client secret or the `private_key_jwt` key, or a moved SSO address, signs the organization out until re-test; with federated-only and one connection the change is impossible, and an expiring credential breaks sign-in with no way out; no overlap rotation and no expiry alert exist. Fix: in-place credential rotation with an overlap, staged pending revisions for endpoint and attribute changes, revoke only on a subject-rule change, expiry alerts.
2. **Major. Overview, Goals, Non-Goals, Decision 013-002, Requirements 013-018, 013-026, Gap 013-011.** "Within seconds" holds only from the provider's SCIM call (Entra ID sends in cycles of about 40 minutes); a connection without SCIM never learns of a disabled person, who keeps the Studio for 12 to 24 hours and the CLI for 7 days, yet back-channel logout was waved away on SCIM's account. Fix: state the bound from the SCIM call with each provider's behaviour; shorter lifetimes, an upstream re-check, or a stated gap for connections without SCIM.
3. **Major. Requirements 013-024, 013-003, 013-033; Technical Plan 9; Goals.** The identity egress guard (`https`, never loopback) cannot reach the development OIDC provider (`http`, loopback) or Keycloak (loopback ports); the deployment verifier has a development escape (`allow_insecure_jwks_http`), the guard none; an ad hoc flag would open the hole the guard closes. Fix: a development-only relaxation in the domain policy, refused at start-up outside `SAGAT_ENV=dev`, with a negative test.
4. **Major. Requirement 013-019; Technical Plan 4 "Verify" and "Signing keys"; Architecture decision 0140 decisions 6 and 7; Risks.** The join matches (`tenant`, `sid`) only, so with a leaked key an attacker who saw any token mints one for any `sub` on that live `sid`; rotation takes about 7 minutes and there is no emergency path; "revocation of sessions" does not help. Fix: join on (`tenant`, `sid`, `subject`, `client_id`), optionally the key id; `identity signing-key revoke`; an emergency runbook.
5. **Major. Requirement 013-021; Technical Plan 13.** The last-administrator guard has write skew: two SCIM suspensions of the last two administrators each hold the settings row `FOR SHARE`, each sees the other active, both commit; the same between SCIM, the member routes and the recompute job. Fix: every write that can lower the active administrators takes the settings row `FOR UPDATE` (or an advisory lock); barrier tests for SCIM against SCIM and SCIM against `PATCH /members`.
6. **Major. Technical Plans 3 and 8.** The deployment-wide cap of 100,000 attempt rows lets one anonymous flood against one slug stop every tenant's sign-ins; the per-tenant priority for live attempt cookies or sessions protects nobody who needs to sign in. Fix: write nothing at authorize (seal the attempt in the cookie, persist at a successful callback), or cap per tenant.
7. **Major. Technical Plan 4 "Lock order"; 029's group-source contract and plan; Non-functional requirement 013-003; Decision 013-018.** Every SCIM request takes the tenant access fence exclusively while applying up to 1,000 member operations and recomputing roles; a queued exclusive lock makes run admissions (shared lockers) wait, so an initial sync stalls admissions repeatedly; no measurement covers it. Fix: exclusive only for changes that matter to authority, a bounded recompute per transaction, an admission-latency benchmark during a replayed sync.
8. **Major. Requirement 013-018; Decision 013-003; Non-functional requirement 013-004; `crates/api-http/src/blobs.rs:129-132`, `crates/api-http/src/routes/database_results.rs:41-51`.** Uploads and database-result reads are bounded by the time left on the caller's token; with five-minute tokens renewed 60 seconds before expiry, a 600-second upload is cut silently. Fix: bound by the session's expiry with a session re-check, or state the bound and renew before large transfers, with tests.
9. **Major. Requirements 013-018, 013-019; Technical Plan 10; Architecture decision 0140; `crates/api-http/src/routes/execution.rs`.** Renewal only after activity, with five-minute tokens, ends a passive viewer's run stream within five minutes and the reconnect has no token; today an hour-long token keeps following. Fix: a visible tab with an open run stream counts as activity, bounded by the absolute expiry; a passive-viewer test.
10. **Nit. Technical Plan 13; Requirements 013-001, 013-010.** Mass revocation under `FOR UPDATE` while every renewal takes `FOR SHARE` on two hot rows; renewal needs no lock since the join refuses. Fix: join the connection state and a revision, revoke by bumping it, drop the renewal locks.
11. **Nit. Technical Plan 4 "Signing keys".** `JwksCache` has three settings (time to live 600 seconds, staleness 900, minimum interval 30); with the defaults a retired key may stay accepted 25 minutes, and "stops signing when older than two intervals" has no state in the cache. Fix: name all three for the platform cache; give the signer its own freshness record.
12. **Nit. Technical Plan 3.** Refusing a tenant without a settings row fails closed even with federation off, breaks test files that insert tenants, and locks out a tenant a 0.2 binary provisions in a mixed fleet. Fix: a left join with defaults, or a trigger on `tenants`.
13. **Nit. Technical Plan 4 "Lock order"; Architecture decision 0050.** The design adds rows to slot 4a, the code to slot 2 and an advisory lock between them without amending Architecture decision 0050's table or listing decision 7 as refined. Fix: amend and list.
14. **Nit. Requirement 013-009; Technical Plan 7.** One login cookie per browser: a second tab or the test window overwrites it and breaks the first attempt. Fix: a cookie per attempt or a small set.
15. **Nit. Requirement 013-017; group-source contract; Requirement 013-031.** "1,000 member operations per patch" against "per SCIM request", with groups of up to 10,000: a full-set request over 1,000 is either refused or unbounded. Fix: one rule and large-group fixtures.
16. **Nit. Constitution Check VII; Technical Plan 6.** Idempotence covers duplicates, not late or reordered requests without `If-Match`. Fix: argue convergence, or refuse stale writes by version.
17. **Nit. Technical Plan 13; Architecture decision 0142 decision 6; Constitution Check V.** The definer functions give the internet-facing process full write power over memberships, including `admin`, where only the schema owner provisioned before; not recorded. Fix: Complexity Tracking and the Constitution V evidence.
18. **Nit. Gap 013-009; Technical Plan 13.** "Constitution 5.0.0, Regions" is not in this worktree (4.7.0); the quote "the runtime deliberately cannot change memberships" is in `20261126000000_authority_read_locks`, not `20261008000200`; Architecture decision 0082 decision 4 says about 12 seconds plus the call, not 10. Fix: correct the citations.
